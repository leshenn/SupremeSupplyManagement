Supreme Supply says it adapts the service to each client's needs.

## Supply Chain Management areas

### Plan
Helping clients meet desired goals through supplier relationships and sourcing strategies.

### Analysis
Analyzing each stage of the supply chain, from raw materials or suppliers through to delivery of the final product.

### Management
Managing the flow of goods and services, including processes that transform raw materials into final products.

### Product
Processing raw materials or components to produce the best possible end product.

### Procurement
Helping ensure that clients obtain the products or services needed to fulfil their business model.

### Logistics
Transportation and storage of goods from the point of origin to the point of consumption.

### Distribution
Handling the flow of goods and services from producers to consumers.

# 20. Content / UX Issues Worth Addressing During the Redesign

These are observations from the current content structure rather than new company claims.

## Page naming / URLs
Many important pages use generic or temporary-looking URLs such as:

- `/page-2/`
- `/page-3/`
- `/page-4/`
- `/page-5/`
- `/page-8/`
- `/page-9/`
- `/page-11/`
- `/page-12/`
- `/about-clone/`
- `/services-clone/`

Recommended future URL structure:

- `/about/`
- `/services/sea-freight/`
- `/services/air-freight/`
- `/services/road-freight/`
- `/services/warehousing/`
- `/services/supply-chain-management/`
- `/projects/`
- `/industries/`
- `/testimonials/`
- `/gallery/`
- `/contact/`

## Important text embedded in images
The About page uses images for major content including:

- About Us
- What We Offer
- Our Offer
- Global Connections

This reduces:

- SEO value
- Accessibility
- Responsive flexibility
- Ease of editing
- Searchability

This information should be converted into normal HTML text in the redesign.

## Inconsistent navigation
Different pages expose slightly different navigation structures. Some show:

- About Us
- Services
- Projects
- Gallery
- Testimonials
- Industry
- Blog
- Contact

Other pages expose only a smaller subset.

The redesign should use one consistent header/navigation system.

## Inconsistent contact email
Some pages include:
- info@supremesupply.co.za

While others also include:
- supremeglobalsoft@gmail.com

This should be standardized.

## Homepage statistics appear broken
The homepage currently renders statistics as:

- `2+ Offices`
- `1+ Co untries`
- `1+ Continents`

These do not match the nearby statement claiming a network of **250 offices in 120 countries across 6 continents**.

This should be corrected or clarified.

## Copy / grammar inconsistencies
Examples worth editing:

- “tailored solution” → likely “tailored solutions”
- “seamless , end- to- end” → spacing/punctuation issue
- “clients need” → likely “clients' need”
- “well packaged service” → likely “well-packaged service”
- “PLan” → capitalization issue
- “S UPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT” → spacing issue
- Sea Freight repeats “Imports” where “Exports” appears intended
- “You are guaranteed of...” could be rewritten more naturally
- “Co untries” appears broken
- Capitalization is inconsistent across headings and footer links

## Industry page
The Industry page currently appears to contain little or no extractable content and should either be:

- Properly populated
- Rebuilt
- Removed from navigation

## Blog listing
The blog index uses **“New Post”** for both articles instead of their actual titles.

## Third-party blog content
At least one article explicitly states that its content is copyright-protected by Now Media.

The content strategy should be reviewed before republishing third-party articles.

## Footer
The copyright year still says **2024**.
